🧠 How the AI Works:

Easy: Uniform random choice. Cannot be exploited, cannot exploit you.

Medium (Frequency Decay): Keeps an exponential moving average of your move choices and counters your favorite habits.

Hard (Markov Model): Remembers 2-step sequences of your moves (e.g., if you play Rock then Paper, it remembers what you played third) to predict and counter your next move!
